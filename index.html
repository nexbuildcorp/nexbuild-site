Scope
Pre-construction through DOB closeout

A general contractor that treats procurement and permitting as the schedule, not paperwork around it.

Most renovations don't slip during construction. They slip while waiting on hardware that was ordered late, a countertop template that was never scheduled, or an inspection that nobody filed for. NexBuild runs those gates as hard as the trades.

We take drawings and field conditions and turn them into purchase-ready scopes, coordinate owners, architects, engineers and subcontractors in writing, and surface schedule risk early enough to do something about it.

Four gates between the drawings and the keys.

Every project runs the same way, whether it's a brownstone kitchen, a 1,200 sq ft suite or a full-floor clinic. The discipline is in what gets locked before anyone mobilizes.

Start a conversation
  1. 01

    Pre-construction

    We walk the space, reconcile the drawings against field conditions, and return a scope-by-scope proposal that separates committed work from allowances, so you know exactly what's priced and what's still a decision.

    • Site survey
    • Scope & allowance definition
    • Schedule with long-lead flags
    • Alternates for review
  2. 02

    Procurement

    Doors and hardware, operators, lighting and controls, solid-surface tops, millwork. We verify quantities against the schedule, confirm lead times in writing, and release purchase orders with fixed delivery dates before the trade needs them.

    • Vendor benchmarking
    • Submittals & approvals
    • Fixed-date purchase orders
    • Template & delivery tracking
  3. 03

    Construction & coordination

    One point of accountability across subcontractors, building management, the design team and the owner. Field questions become written RFIs. Changes become documented change orders before the work happens, not after.

    • Trade sequencing
    • Owner & architect coordination
    • Written RFIs and change orders
    • Weekly look-ahead
  4. 04

    Closeout & turnover

    Inspections scheduled early, documentation assembled as the work is built, punch list run to zero. You get a space that passes, and a closeout package you can hand to your facilities team.

    • DOB & special inspections
    • Mechanical & energy-code sign-off
    • Air-balance reports
    • Punch & O&M package

Why owners and architects work with us

Fewer surprises. Written down.

Schedule-first procurement

We don't take a supplier's lead time on faith. Quantities get checked against the door and fixture schedules, delivery dates go into the purchase order, and anything that threatens the turnover date gets an approved alternate on the table before it becomes a delay.

Complete packages, not low numbers

A cheap quote that's missing the closers, the operator header or the template visit isn't cheap. We bid and buy the whole installed scope.

Early, honest risk calls

If a delivery is at risk, you hear it from us first, with options, not after the date has passed.

Clean closeout

Inspection documents, balance reports and sign-offs are collected as the work goes in, so the end of the job is a walk-through, not a scramble.

One accountable contact

Owners, designers, engineers, building management and vendors all route through one coordinator who keeps the written record straight.
